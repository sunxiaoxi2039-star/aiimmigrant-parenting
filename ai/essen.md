### 问题

吃饭与食品安全

> 「亚超买回来的东西到底能不能给孩子吃？还是说德国这边什么都生吃才更让人担心？」

### 引言

一位家长觉得做熟才稳妥，另一位觉得不该对一整个货架笼统地不放心。这里常常是担心对担心，不是证据对证据。

### 场景

周二傍晚六点多，米娅（4岁）用叉子把西兰花拨到盘子边上，米饭早就吃光了。

乔纳斯用德语说：「先吃完，再吃甜点」，手指在她盘子旁边点了点桌面。

- 两个人都希望孩子吃得安全、营养够。
- 两个人都认同蔬果多样、少糖少盐。
- 婴幼儿在生食和高风险食物上本来就该区别对待——这不是哪国菜的问题。

#### 1. 尽量做熟，还是也可以吃生的？

- A（成立条件）：对象是婴幼儿、孕妇或免疫力较弱的人时，充分加热是更稳妥的选择。
- B（成立条件）：对象是健康的大孩子和成人时，认真清洗的生蔬是营养来源，本身不构成风险。

### Real disputes (not adjudicated)

#### 1. Must the child finish the plate before leaving the table?

- One side: Whoever sits at the table eats what was served; finishing gives the meal a firm frame.
- The other side: The child decides whether to eat, and how much, of what the adults provide (this is how the Satter model describes it).
- The official pages we read say nothing on this. We have no checked source for the finish-the-plate rule; it is described here as a position, not as evidence.

- One side: Dessert comes after the main course, so the vegetables are not skipped.
- The other side: Sweets are not tied to eating or not eating.
- BIÖG only writes to go sparingly with sugar, sweets and snack products; whether dessert should depend on finishing is not addressed by the page we read.

#### 3. Does “raw” apply across the board – or only to certain foods?

- One side: For small children it is better to keep everything cooked and familiar; then there is less to decide case by case.
- The other side: Fresh vegetables and fruit belong on the plate; each food is looked at on its own.
- The official bodies name individual foods: BIÖG raw root vegetables (because of choking, also in pieces), BfR raw foods of animal origin (only well heated). For questions about a particular food, your paediatrician can help.
